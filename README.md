# Year 12–13 IB Diploma Economics (SL & HL — FA2022) Study Guide

Interactive study guide, procedural quantitative drill engine, mock-exam suite, Formula & 9-Concepts Toolkit, and 3-Commentary Portfolio IA guide for **IB Diploma Economics (First Assessment 2022)** — covering both DP1 and DP2 across Units 1–4 (`1.1–4.10`) plus HL Paper 3 with a live **SL / HL toggle**.

## Highlights
- **8 Comprehensive IB Topics (`1.1–4.10` + HL Paper 3):** Full SL + HL coverage threading all **9 Key Concepts** (*Scarcity, Choice, Efficiency, Equity, Economic well-being, Sustainability, Change, Interdependence, Intervention*) with HL-only extensions and Paper 3 tagged `level: "HL"`.
- **Official IBO FA2022 Assessment Blueprint:** HL **Paper 1 (20%) · Paper 2 (30%) · Paper 3 (30%) · IA (20%)** and SL **Paper 1 (30%) · Paper 2 (40%) · IA (30%)** — with Paper 1 Part (b) real-world examples, Paper 2 unseen data response, HL Paper 3 quantitative + 10-mark policy recommendations, and a 25-Q Diagnostic MCQ Bank.
- **Interactive Economics Explorables:** Custom SVG simulators per topic (PPC & circular flow, supply-demand/tax/subsidy surplus & DWL, externality MSC/MSB & oligopoly Nash payoff matrix [HL], AD/AS Monetarist vs Keynesian & Phillips Curve [HL], Keynesian multiplier [HL], tariff/quota welfare & comparative advantage [HL], Forex & J-curve [HL], and Paper 3 policy workbench [HL]).
- **IA Portfolio of 3 Commentaries (`/ia`):** Complete Criteria A–E (14m × 3) + Criterion F (3m) = 45 marks guide, 800-word budget, 3-unit/3-concept/3-source compliance checklist.
- **Zero-Login iPad Persistence:** All progress, Daily 5, SRS review, streaks, and SL/HL selection save automatically in `localStorage`.
